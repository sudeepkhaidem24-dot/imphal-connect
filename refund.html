Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Subscription cancellation

Business owners may request cancellation of a Pro or Elite subscription through the account/business controls where enabled. Cancellation stops the subscription from continuing according to the applicable Razorpay subscription state. Access already paid for may continue until the recorded subscription end date.

Refunds

Because subscription access is a digital service, fees are generally non-refundable once the paid service has been activated and made available. We may consider a refund where a payment was duplicated, a subscription was successfully charged but could not be activated because of an Imphal Connect technical failure, or another refund is required by applicable law.

How to request help

When requesting a refund, provide the account email, business name, payment date, amount and Razorpay payment/subscription reference if available. Do not send card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or passwords.

Processing

Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method through the payment processor. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account can depend on the payment provider and bank.

Policy display

This policy is displayed before or alongside access to paid subscription information and is part of the Imphal Connect Terms of Use.
