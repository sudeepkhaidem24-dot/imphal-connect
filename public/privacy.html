Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Imphal Connect collects information needed to operate accounts, business profiles, customer enquiries, analytics and subscriptions.

Information we may collect

Account information such as name and email; business information such as business name, category, contact details and location; customer enquiry details submitted to a business; and technical information needed for security and service operation.

How information is used

We use information to provide and improve the platform, authenticate users, deliver business tools, process subscriptions, provide customer support, prevent abuse and communicate important service information.

Payments

Payment information is processed by our payment service provider. Imphal Connect does not intentionally store complete card credentials on its own application servers.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers necessary to operate the platform, comply with law, protect users and process payments. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards and retain information only as needed for legitimate business, legal and security purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact & Support page.
