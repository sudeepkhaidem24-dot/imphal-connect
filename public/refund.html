Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Subscription cancellation

Businesses may cancel a paid subscription using the available account cancellation controls. Cancellation prevents future renewal; access normally continues through the already-paid subscription period.

Refunds

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable after a billing period has started, except where a refund is required by applicable law or where a duplicate, erroneous or unauthorized charge is confirmed. Approved refunds are returned through the payment method or process permitted by the payment provider.

Payment disputes

If you believe a payment was made in error, contact us promptly with the account email, transaction reference and relevant details so the issue can be reviewed.

Contact

For cancellation or refund requests, use the Contact & Support page.
