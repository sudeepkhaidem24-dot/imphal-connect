Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may process account details such as name and email, business profile information, customer enquiry details, uploaded business content, subscription information and technical/analytics events.

How we use information

We use information to provide accounts, business profiles, discovery, enquiries, subscriptions, analytics, security, fraud prevention, support and service improvements.

Payments

Payment processing is handled by Razorpay. Imphal Connect is not intended to store card numbers, UPI PINs, CVV values or other payment-instrument credentials on its own servers.

Service providers

We use infrastructure and service providers such as Supabase for application data/authentication and Razorpay for payment processing. Information is shared only as needed to provide the relevant service, maintain security or comply with law.

Customer enquiries

If you submit an enquiry to a business, the information you choose to provide is sent to that business so it can contact you about your request.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including authenticated server-side operations and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request help regarding your account or personal information through the platform's support channel. We may retain information where necessary for security, legal, accounting or legitimate operational purposes.
