Contact & Support

Imphal Connect is operated for customers and local businesses in Imphal, Manipur, India.

Customer support

For account, subscription, payment or business-listing support, use the support/contact channel made available inside the Imphal Connect application. Include your account email, business name and relevant payment/reference ID when contacting us.

Operating location

Imphal, Manipur, India.

Payment support

Subscription payments are processed by Razorpay. Never share your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or account password with support.

Business support

Business owners can manage their profile, catalogue, enquiries and subscription from the business controls after signing in.
